# QUORUM — 90-Second Demo Video Script

**Target Duration**: 1 minute 30 seconds (90s)  
**Tone**: Direct, energetic, builder-focused, professional  
**Presenter Setup**: Screen recording of QUORUM web interface at `http://localhost:8000`.

---

## Timeline & Scene Progression

### [0:00 – 0:15] The Hook: The Polite Lie Problem
- **Visual**: Camera or clean title card transitioning immediately to the QUORUM landing screen. Clean dark glassmorphic UI, glowing live telemetry badge.
- **Presenter**: 
  > *"Every early-stage founder faces the same fatal trap: friends and mentors give polite encouragement, and generic AI gives sycophantic praise. You spend 6 months and your entire savings building something nobody actually wants. This is QUORUM: an adversarial pre-mortem co-pilot that stress-tests your startup idea before you write a single line of code."*

---

### [0:15 – 0:35] Pitch Input & Context Injection
- **Visual**: 
  - Type or paste idea: *"AegisHealth AI: Ambient clinical intelligence for solo community clinic doctors. Transcribes patient encounters into SOAP notes and flags formulary contraindications in real time."*
  - Click Context Pills: `Stage: Prototype`, `Market: Healthcare B2B`, `Team: Solo Founder`, `Runway: Bootstrapped`, `Goal: Validate Urgency`.
  - Highlight the pitch deck upload button (.pdf, .docx, .md).
- **Presenter**: 
  > *"Unlike generic prompts, QUORUM factors in founder constraints. A bootstrapped solo founder needs ruthless distribution clarity, not multi-million dollar enterprise advice. We click 'Convene Council'."*

---

### [0:35 – 0:55] The Adversarial Council & Live Streaming
- **Visual**: 
  - Show the 5–7 expert cards appearing via Server-Sent Events (SSE).
  - Reveal personas selected deterministically based on sector and context: Paul Graham (Early-Stage Product), Vinod Khosla (Deep Tech Skeptic), Chamath Palihapitiya (Unit Economics Hawk), and Naval Ravikant (Contrarian).
  - As each expert renders, highlight their pillar scores (0–10) and their brutal **Kill Question**.
- **Presenter**: 
  > *"In seconds, QUORUM queries its catalog of 500+ grounded industry icons and routes 6 adversarial evaluators. Watch live: Paul Graham probes user obsession, while Chamath scrutinizes customer acquisition payback. Each expert asks one lethal kill question targeting the founder's weakest blind spot."*

---

### [0:55 – 1:15] Deep Pre-Mortem Report & Tension Graph
- **Visual**: 
  - Scroll through the synthesized report: Overall Score (`5.7/10 - Promising but risky`), 8 Pillars Radar/Breakdown, Top Existential Risks.
  - Hover over the **2D Interactive Consensus & Tension Graph**: nodes positioned by stance, color-coded edges showing where experts violently disagree.
  - Show the **Tactical 30-60-90 Day Roadmap** and **Tailored Next Actions for Solo Bootstrapped Founders**.
- **Presenter**: 
  > *"The synthesizer extracts collective consensus. We see high feasibility, but execution risk and defensibility drag the venture down. The 2D tension graph maps where our panel disagrees. Most importantly, the roadmap gives concrete, day-by-day validation actions tailored to our exact runway."*

---

### [1:15 – 1:30] The Live Rebuttal Loop & Honest Offline Core
- **Visual**: 
  - Click "Rebut" on Paul Graham's kill question: *"Why won't doctors just use Nuance or Epic?"*
  - Type defense: *"Solo rural clinics can't afford Epic's $50k upfront setup fee; we offer zero-integration browser audio at $79/mo."*
  - Click "Submit Rebuttal": Watch score update dynamically.
  - Show offline badge toggled: app works 100% offline with zero dependencies or API keys.
- **Presenter**: 
  > *"Founders don't just read the verdict—they defend their thesis. Submit a rebuttal, and the council recalculates your defensibility live. QUORUM runs in under 60 KB, with zero frontend build steps, and full offline resilience. Stop building on polite lies. Run QUORUM first."*
